How to make a short-form video with your visualizer

Shown in Edits, Instagram's free video editing app. Red circles show what to tap. Get Edits on the App Store · Get Edits on Google Play

  1. Add your footageOpen Edits, start a new project and add your background clip.
  2. Add the visualizerTap Overlay, choose Gallery and pick your visualizer MP4.
  3. Blend it inTap the layer style button, then lower Opacity (or set Blending to Screen) so your footage shows through.
  4. Export and shareTap Next, then post to Instagram or tap Download.
